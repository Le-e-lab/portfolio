## Problem

Small-ticket credit gets gated on paperwork most borrowers do not have: a
payslip, a formal bank account, something to pledge. Lenders score on paper, so
the applicants carrying the least documentation get priced out instead of
assessed.

They are not unbanked, though. They already transact, over USSD and mobile
wallets, because that is where the money actually moves for them. The gap is not
access to financial services. It is that the lenders serving this market will
not underwrite a customer who cannot produce a document trail.

## Process

Three things had to work as a single product: microloans, buy-now-pay-later,
and the financial tooling around them. Underwriting was the hard part, because
the applicants arrive with the thinnest paper trail of any customer a lender
normally sees.

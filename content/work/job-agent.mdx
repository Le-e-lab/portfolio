## Problem

Job alerts arrive in the same inbox as everything else, and the deadline is
usually shorter than the attention they deserve. By the time a promising posting
has been read and judged, the applicants who needed no thought are already in.
The loss is rarely a job that was winnable. It is the ones that never got an
application at all.

The second problem is quieter. An application written in a hurry reads like an
application written in a hurry, and the reader can tell. More applications is
not the fix. Arriving earlier, and arriving with something tailored, is.

## Process

The agent watches the inbox, decides what actually matters, and pushes only the
urgent items out of email and onto WhatsApp, where there is a chance of acting
on them before the posting closes.
